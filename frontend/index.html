Copy-trading strategies

Sharpe, volatility and max drawdown are computed from real NAV history with SQL window functions and served from a materialized view. When a leader fills, a trigger enqueues a fan-out job that workers drain with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED — the leader's order is never blocked by a follower's risk check.
